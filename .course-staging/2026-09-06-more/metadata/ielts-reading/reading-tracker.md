# IELTS Reading Tracker

## Procedure For Each IELTS Reading PDF

1. Do a 2-minute skim: title, first/last paragraph, topic sentences.
2. Read questions and underline keywords.
3. Scan the passage for keyword areas.
4. Answer under time pressure.
5. Check answers.
6. Record score, wrong-answer type, and reason.
7. Extract useful vocabulary only after checking the score.
